Hey, I'm Nasma! 👋 I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Université Laval, working on NLP for low-resource languages — specifically machine translation for Coptic and Quebec French. I explore different strategies to keep models accurate and robust when clean training data is scarce.

I'm increasingly drawn to a broader question: how can language models reason about knowledge in ways that are reliable and verifiable, not just fluent-sounding. It's a direction I'd like to keep exploring going forward.

I also hold a Bachelor's in Software Engineering and am a Candidate to the Engineering Profession (CEP) in Quebec.

---

### 🔭 Featured Research

**Neural Machine Translation for Coptic–French: Strategies for Low-Resource Ancient Languages**
📝 Published at LT4HALA @ LREC 2026

Direct vs. pivot translation · pretrained model selection · multi-version fine-tuning · robustness to manuscript noise — for an extremely low-resource, extinct-language pair. Two fine-tuned models released on Hugging Face.
