### 🔭 Featured Research

**[coptic–french-nmt](https://github.com/chaouin/coptic-french-nmt)** — Neural Machine Translation for Coptic–French: Strategies for Low-Resource Ancient Languages
Investigates direct vs. pivot translation, pretrained model choice, multi-version fine-tuning, and **robustness to manuscript noise** for an extremely low-resource, extinct-language pair. Two fine-tuned models released on Hugging Face.
📄 [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10683) · 🤗 [Models](https://huggingface.co/chaouin)

> The core problem here — building reliable models when clean training data barely exists and inputs are noisy or shifted from what the model saw in training — is the same problem underlying simulation-based inference in astrophysics, where models trained on simulations have to generalize to noisy real observations. That link is the throughline across my work.
